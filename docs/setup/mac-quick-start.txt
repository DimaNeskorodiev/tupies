Tupies for Mac — quick start

Apple Silicon (M-series), macOS 14 or later.

1. Download the notarized Tupies.dmg from the official Tupies release.
2. Open it and drag Tupies into Applications. Eject the disk image.
3. Open Tupies from Applications. Choose Use this Mac → Start my engine.
4. Follow Continue setup: connect your own eligible ChatGPT / Codex account,
   provider API key, or separately configured local model.
5. Meet Chief of Staff and Ops, choose an example, and press Send.

No Terminal, Xcode, Homebrew, Docker or separate Node.js installation is needed.
Cloud models need internet access and use your provider's limits and billing.
Browser actions additionally require Chrome, Chromium or Edge on this Mac.

Your bots, chats and files belong to your own workspace on this Mac.
Closing the window keeps Tupies running. Quit (Command-Q) stops the engine
started by Tupies. Keep the Mac awake for background tasks and routines.

Updates: finish or stop active tasks, quit Tupies, then replace the app in
Applications with the new notarized version. Reopen it. Your workspace lives
separately in ~/Library/Application Support/Tupies/Engine and stays in place.
Do not remove that folder. Keep a protected backup before updating; see
Settings → Help → Backups & operations. There is no automatic updater yet.

If macOS says the developer cannot be verified or the app is damaged, stop
and obtain the official notarized release. Do not disable Gatekeeper.

Downloads: https://github.com/DimaNeskorodiev/tupies/releases
Support: https://github.com/DimaNeskorodiev/tupies/issues

Tupies-UNSIGNED.dmg is an internal packaging candidate, not a release for
normal installation. It has not passed the distribution gates.
